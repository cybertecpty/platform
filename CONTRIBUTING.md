## Contributor License Agreement

Outside contributions require agreement to the [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md). It
lets the Company distribute your contribution under the project license **and** under other
license terms, such as a commercial or subscription offering. You keep the copyright in your
work.

By opening a pull request you confirm that you have read and agree to the CLA. Tick the CLA
box in the pull request template. If you contribute for an employer, make sure you are
authorized to agree on its behalf.

We cannot merge a contribution without that confirmation.
